Audi A8 Night Vision

An explainer animation for the Audi A8 night vision assistant, showing how the system detects a pedestrian and alerts the driver.

Role
Creative Director, agency:collective
Client
Audi
Year
2010
Scope
3D explainer animation

The problem

The files centre on a short animation explaining the A8 night vision assistant, a feature that detects pedestrians beyond the reach of the headlights.

The approach

The animation follows an A8 from above on a dark country road. Colour-coded zones project ahead of the car — blue within the headlight beam, yellow for the wider detection field, and red for the area where a pedestrian becomes a risk.

When a pedestrian enters the red zone, an inset of the instrument-cluster display shows the thermal image with the figure highlighted, alongside audible and visual warning icons.

Leadership

Directed the studio's work on the animation.
