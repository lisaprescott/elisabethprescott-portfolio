The logo

We started with the mark. The logo and wordmark workshop explored dozens of directions, from pure wordmarks to abstract symbols, before narrowing to one idea: forward momentum.

The final mark, The Path, is three interconnected parts that shift at every point to create rhythm. As it thickens it reads as free-flowing electricity, the energy of being somewhere in person. That electricity became the thread for the rest of the system.

Illustration

Two kinds, both used for surprise and delight. Metaphorical spot illustrations explain specific needs in a light, whimsical style, with recurring details instead of a mascot and portals as the metaphor for Eventbrite as a gateway to new experiences.
